Quick guide to looksmaxxing your way to genetic glory

1

ROLL THE DICE — Click "ROLL GENETICS" to randomize your starting stats. Height, jaw, tilt, and hairline are permanent; the rest can be improved.

2

YEARLY ACTIONS — Each year you get 10 AP. Spend them on gym, skincare, surgeries, dating, career, substances, or social media. Plan wisely.

3

STATS & SMV — Your SMV rating (1.0–8.0) determines your social tier. Higher stats = better career, dating, and battle outcomes.

4

RISK & REWARD — Surgeries and substances can boost stats fast but carry risk of botching, addiction, or death. Don't gamble what you can't lose.

5

REINCARNATION — Die or reach Age 50 to earn Cope Tokens. Spend them in the Shop on permanent perks for future runs.

6

CARD BATTLES — In the Encounters tab, fight social opponents using cards unlocked by your stats. Play cards to reduce opponent Skepticism before your Confidence runs out. Chain cards for COMBO effects (e.g., Jawline Flash after Nice Personality = 2x damage!).

7

DATING & SYNERGIES — Swipe on profiles in Dating Apps. Match chance depends on SMV, height, tilt, and skin. Stat Synergies (e.g., Chiseled Jaw + Broad Frame = Chad Frame) unlock passive bonuses when you level up multiple stats together.

8

GYM MEMBERSHIP — Buy a gym membership ($500) from the Year Actions for cheaper gym sessions and +1-3 passive Frame gain each year. Combine with the Gym Rat talent for even faster gains.
